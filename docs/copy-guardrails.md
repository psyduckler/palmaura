# PalmAura Copy Guardrails

PalmAura must be framed as symbolic entertainment and self-reflection only.

## Required framing

- Entertainment only
- Symbolic
- Self-reflection
- May suggest / may symbolize / invites reflection
- Not a substitute for professional advice

## Banned claim categories

Do not generate or ship copy that claims or implies:

- Medical advice, diagnosis, disease, illness, treatment, or health outcomes
- Psychological diagnosis or mental health advice
- Legal advice or legal outcomes
- Financial advice, guaranteed wealth, or investment outcomes
- Fertility, pregnancy, death, lifespan, or physical harm predictions
- Guaranteed relationships, exact future events, or unavoidable fate
- Certainty that the app can truly know the user's destiny

## Image validation

If an image is not clearly a human palm/open hand, the backend must return a friendly rejection instead of generating a fake reading.
