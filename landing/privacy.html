Privacy Policy

PalmAura offers symbolic palm readings for entertainment and self-reflection only.

Palm photos

PalmAura does not store your palm photos on our servers. Images are sent to our AI provider for real-time processing and are not saved by PalmAura.

Data we may collect

  • Device identifier for rate limiting and abuse prevention.
  • Usage events such as reading requested, reading completed, and share tapped if analytics are enabled.
  • Crash diagnostics if crash reporting is enabled.

We do not require an account and do not collect your name, email, contacts, or location by default.

AI provider

Images are transmitted to an AI provider to generate the reading. The provider's processing and retention terms may apply while handling the request.
