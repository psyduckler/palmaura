Privacy Policy — PalmAura

Effective date: May 21, 2026

PalmAura is an iOS app that provides symbolic palm readings for entertainment and self-reflection only.

What PalmAura collects

  • Palm photo: sent to PalmAura's backend at palmaura.app for reading generation. PalmAura does not retain palm photos server-side after the reading is generated.
  • Reading personalization: birthday, gender, dominant hand, reading style, life season, and optional location/place text you provide.
  • Per-reading question: if you type a question, it is sent only to shape that specific reading and never appears on share cards.
  • Coarse location context: city, region, country, and timezone may be inferred from Cloudflare edge context. PalmAura does not request iOS Location permission and does not use GPS.
  • Device identifier: an app/device identifier is used for rate limiting and abuse prevention. Raw device IDs are minimized before storage in rate-limit keys.

What stays on your device

Reading history, saved palm photos, and per-reading questions are stored in the app's private on-device storage. You can clear them from Settings → Clear Reading History. Uninstalling the app removes local app data.

AI processing

Palm photos and reading context are sent to an AI provider to generate the reading. PalmAura does not use this data to identify you, sell data, or track you across apps or websites.

Website analytics

The PalmAura marketing website may use basic web analytics to understand page views and site performance. This is separate from the iOS app. The iOS app does not ship Google Analytics, Sentry, PostHog, advertising SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

What PalmAura does not do

  • No account required.
  • No sale of data.
  • No cross-app tracking.
  • No IDFA / ATT prompt.
  • No iOS Location, Contacts, Health, Calendar, or microphone permission.
  • No medical, legal, financial, psychological, fertility, pregnancy, lifespan, death, or life-critical advice.
