PalmAura Support

PalmAura is a symbolic palm-reading app for entertainment and self-reflection. For help, email hello@palmaura.app.

Common questions

The app says my photo is not a palm.

Use soft, even light. Open your hand with fingers spread. Fill the frame with your palm and keep the photo sharp.

Do I need an account?

No. PalmAura does not require an account.

Is this medical, legal, financial, or psychological advice?

No. PalmAura is symbolic entertainment and self-reflection only. It is not professional advice.

How do I delete my data?

Use Settings → Clear Reading History to remove on-device readings, palm photos, and per-reading questions. You can also uninstall the app to remove local app data.
